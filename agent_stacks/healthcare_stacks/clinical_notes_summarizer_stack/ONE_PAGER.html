AGENTIC PROCESS FLOW — 8 STEPS
1Encounter Recording
Clinician records the patient encounter via voice dictation into the Power Apps clinical workspace, or uses ambient AI listening during the consultation.
Power AppsAzure AI Speech
2Speech-to-Text Transcription
Azure AI Speech Services converts the audio into a structured transcript in real time, identifying speaker roles (clinician vs. patient).
Azure AI Speech
3Clinical Note Structuring
Azure OpenAI structures the transcript into SOAP format (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan) and extracts key clinical entities.
Azure OpenAICopilot Studio
4ICD & CPT Code Suggestion
Copilot Studio matches extracted clinical entities against ICD-10/11 and CPT code libraries, suggesting the most accurate codes with confidence scores.
Copilot StudioAzure AI
5Clinician Review & Approval
The structured note and suggested codes are presented to the clinician in Power Apps for rapid review, amendment, and one-click approval.
Power Apps
6EHR Write-Back
Approved clinical note and codes are written back to the EHR System automatically, maintaining the complete longitudinal patient record.
EHR SystemPower Automate
7Charge Capture to Billing
Approved CPT codes and encounter details are passed to the Revenue Cycle Management system to generate the claim, with supporting documentation attached.
Power Automate
8Audit Trail & Compliance Log
Every AI suggestion, clinician acceptance or override, and system action is logged in an immutable audit trail for compliance, quality, and training.
Azure MonitorPower BI

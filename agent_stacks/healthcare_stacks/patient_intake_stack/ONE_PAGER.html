1Patient Contact & Channel Identification
Patient contacts via web portal, mobile app, phone, or walk-in; Copilot Studio identifies the channel and initiates a structured intake conversation.
Copilot Studio
2Identity Verification & Record Matching
Copilot Studio verifies the patient's identity against the Patient Administration System and retrieves their existing medical record if available.
Copilot StudioPatient Administration System
3Symptom & History Capture
A guided conversational assessment captures presenting symptoms, duration, severity, relevant medical history, allergies, and current medications.
Copilot StudioAzure OpenAI
4Clinical Triage Scoring
Azure AI applies validated triage protocols (e.g., Manchester Triage System) to the captured data and generates a priority score with clinical rationale.
Azure AICopilot Studio
5Clinician Alert & Queue Assignment
Power Automate notifies the duty clinician of high-priority patients in real time; the patient is placed in the appropriate care queue in Power Apps.
Power AutomatePower Apps
6Registration Form Pre-Population
Power Apps pre-populates the registration form using the collected data, presenting it for review and digital signature on a tablet or mobile device.
Power Apps
7Insurance & Eligibility Verification
Copilot Studio triggers an automated eligibility check against the payer system and flags coverage gaps or pre-authorisation requirements to admissions staff.
Copilot StudioPayer / Insurance System
